Privacy policy

Your Minecraft data stays yours.

Effective 7 November 2026. This policy describes the Windows desktop application Hyperix Launcher and its official distribution pages.

Who we are

Hyperix is an independent project developed and run by Hadeverl4. For privacy questions or requests, email hyperix.contact@gmail.com.

What Hyperix keeps on your PC

Hyperix keeps launcher settings, instance details, game files, screenshots, logs and account display details on your PC. Sign-in credentials for Microsoft/Minecraft and other supported accounts are stored separately, protected by Windows user-scoped DPAPI. They are never kept in the app's interface data, regular account files, screenshots or launcher logs.

Services Hyperix connects to

Hyperix connects directly to the services below. Some connections start only when you use a feature. Others happen automatically, as noted. Each service can see your network address and processes data under its own policy.

  • Microsoft, Xbox, Minecraft and Mojang: sign-in, play, game metadata and assets.
  • Modrinth and, where enabled, CurseForge: browsing and downloading content. Modrinth also supplies part of the server list. Project descriptions can load images and videos from hosts chosen by their authors.
  • Ely.by: only when you select that provider.
  • Eclipse Temurin (Adoptium) and GitHub: downloading a Java runtime when a game version needs one that is not available.
  • GitHub: automatically, to load Hyperix's server and skin catalogs and, in direct-install builds, to check for updates when the app starts and every 30 minutes. Microsoft Store builds update through the Store.
  • Minecraft servers: automatically, when a server card is on screen Hyperix checks that server's status, so its operator can see your network address.
  • Discord: on by default while the Discord desktop app is running. Your Discord profile can show your Hyperix status, Minecraft version and loader. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • mclo.gs: only when you choose to share a log. Anyone with the link can read it. Hyperix filters known credentials, email addresses, IPv4 addresses and personal folder names, but player names, chat and other details can remain, so review the log first.

Technical data (telemetry)

The Help improve Hyperix setting is on by default. Hyperix sends technical data only while this setting is on, and only in builds connected to a collection server. That server runs on Cloudflare. Events are kept for 14 days. Your network address is used only to rate-limit requests and is never stored. Only these events are sent: app start, Minecraft launch attempt, game exit, a failure while waiting on the game, and a rate-limited failure summary containing only a fixed operation group, action and error category. They can include the app version, operating system and architecture, a temporary session ID, Minecraft version and loader, launch result and duration, and the game's exit status.

Telemetry never includes account names, authentication tokens/codes, instance IDs or names, server/world names, mod lists, file paths, raw logs, screenshots or play-time history. You can turn the setting off in Settings → Privacy. After that, no more telemetry is sent.

In builds that support bug reports, a report is sent only when you agree to send it. It includes your description and the diagnostic details shown in the dialog. To limit spam, the server keeps a coded value made from your connection address for up to 48 hours. The value changes daily, and the address itself is not stored. While Help improve Hyperix is on, up to 256 diagnostic events are kept in memory for the current session. Stable builds send them only with a report you approve, and turning the setting off clears them. Reports are deleted after 14 days. Please do not include passwords or other private information in your description.

The Hyperix website

Opening the website connects your browser to these services, which receive your network address and usual browser details: Mux (hero video), jsDelivr and cdnjs (open-source scripts), GitHub (release lists) and Cloudflare (analytics). Fonts are served from the Hyperix website itself.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits and measures page performance. According to Cloudflare, it does not use cookies or browser storage for this and does not fingerprint visitors. Cloudflare receives your network address and technical data about each page view, such as the page address, referring site, browser type and approximate country, and processes it under its own privacy policy. This analytics is on the website only. The Hyperix Launcher app does not include it.

The website itself sets no cookies. It keeps your language choice in your browser's local storage and a session marker that skips the intro on later page loads. Links to the Microsoft Store, GitHub, Modrinth, CurseForge, Buy Me a Coffee and Ko-fi take you to those services, which handle your data under their own policies. Donations are processed by those platforms, and Hyperix does not receive your payment details.

Hyperix Connect

Hyperix Connect is the account for friends and online play, in versions of Hyperix that include it. It is separate from your Minecraft accounts. You can play with a Microsoft or Ely.by account without it, but adding a Hyperix Play (offline) profile requires signing in to Hyperix Connect. You sign in with Microsoft, and the service runs on Supabase. It handles:

  • Your account: your email address and Microsoft sign-in identifier, your Hyperix ID (name and tag), a permanent public ID, and your privacy choices. Friends and people you exchange requests with can see your name, tag and ID.
  • Friends: your friends list, friend requests and the players you block, with the reason you chose. If you also report a player, we receive your account, theirs and that reason, keep the report for 90 days and use it only to review the complaint.
  • Online status: while you are connected, friends can see whether you are online or playing, with the instance name, game version, loader, player name and skin. This is sent live and is not saved. You can appear offline in Settings.
  • Playing together: a temporary device key, invitations, room records and connection details, which expire after a short time. Invitations and connection details are encrypted between launchers.
  • Connections: players first try to connect directly, so people in your room can see your public IP address. To find that address, Hyperix contacts STUN servers run by Cloudflare and Google, which can see your IP address. If a direct connection fails, encrypted game traffic passes through a relay server run by Hyperix, reached directly or through Tailscale.
  • Usage counts: while you are signed in, Hyperix tells a server run by Hyperix every 20 seconds that the app is online. It sends a random ID that changes each time the app starts, whether you are hosting a room, how many connections are direct, relayed or failed, how much traffic was relayed and why a relay was needed. The server checks your sign-in so each account is counted once, holds your account ID only in memory while you are online and saves only totals. It is reached through Tailscale. This is part of using Connect and stops when you sign out.

To delete your Connect account, open Profile in Settings and choose Delete Hyperix Connect account. It is disabled at once: friends can no longer see or find you, and you leave their lists. For 14 days you can sign in and restore it. After that, your account and its Connect data are permanently deleted. This does not remove skin contributions or data held by other services. Email us about those.

Skin contributions

Contributing a skin on the website is optional. You sign in with Microsoft through Supabase, which receives your email address and Microsoft account identifier. The contribution page keeps its sign-in session in your browser tab. The Hyperix contribution server, which the project operates, stores your account identifier, the contributor name you enter, the skin image, its title and model, and the review status and dates. Reviewers can see your contributor name and image.

Unfinished uploads are removed after 1 day and pending submissions expire after 30 days. A rejected or expired image is deleted, and its record is removed after 90 days. An approved skin's image and title are published in Hyperix's public GitHub repository and shown in the launcher. Your contributor name is not part of the public catalog. We can remove a skin from the catalog on request, but copies can remain in public Git history.

What Hyperix does not do

Hyperix does not sell personal information, show advertising, run an in-app payment system or re-host third-party mod files. It does not use your Minecraft worlds, mods, screenshots or logs as telemetry.

How long data stays

Launcher and game data stays on your PC until you delete it in Hyperix, remove an account or instance, or uninstall and delete the Hyperix data folder. Automatic cleanup in the Storage Manager leaves worlds, mods, screenshots, accounts and Minecraft runtime files alone.

Your choices and requests

You can turn off telemetry and Discord Activity in Settings and remove local data as described above. You can delete a Hyperix Connect account yourself as described above. To ask what data Hyperix holds about you, or to correct or delete it, including a skin contribution and its sign-in account, email hyperix.contact@gmail.com. Telemetry and bug reports are not linked to your account, so we may not be able to identify yours. They are deleted after 14 days. You may have further rights under the law where you live.

Children

Hyperix is not designed specifically for children. Where the law requires it, a child should use Hyperix, and especially the skin contribution page, only with the permission of a parent or guardian. Before the first sign-in, Hyperix Connect asks you to confirm that you are at least 13 and old enough under the law where you live, or that you have permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data without that permission, contact us and we will delete it.

International processing

The services named on this page and the Hyperix contribution server can process data in countries other than yours, which may have different data protection rules.

Security and contact

Hyperix uses Windows to protect sign-in credentials, limits what the app can do on your system, and checks downloads when the provider supplies a hash. No software can guarantee that third-party game content is safe. For support or privacy questions, visit the Hyperix support page or email hyperix.contact@gmail.com. We will update this policy before making a significant change to how data is handled.
